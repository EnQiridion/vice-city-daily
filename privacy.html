Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Vice City Daily ("the app") is a private automation tool used only by the operator of the Vice City Daily YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

The app uses YouTube API Services. With the operator's consent it requests the youtube.upload permission, which is used solely to upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channel. The app does not read, collect or store data about any other YouTube user.

From Twitch, the app reads public clip information (clip title, streamer name, view count and clip URL) through the Twitch API to choose and credit clips.

How data is stored

The OAuth access token is stored only on the operator's own server and is never shared, sold or sent to third parties. The app keeps a list of already-uploaded Twitch clip IDs to avoid duplicates. No personal data, cookies or analytics are used.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

Third-party policies

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
